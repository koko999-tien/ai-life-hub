          ## 5. Monthly Close Checklist (10 Bước - 45p)
          1. **Bank Reconciliation**: Mọi transaction trên bank statement có trong tracker
          2. **Revenue Recognition**: Accrual basis - Revenue khi session completed (không phải khi nhận tiền)
          3. **Expense Capture**: Tất cả receipts/invoices đã log, categorize đúng TK
          4. **Mentor Payout Verification**: Mọi payout đã transfer, mentor confirm received
          5. **Platform Fee Recording**: Stripe/Momo/VNPay fees tách riêng (TK 627)
          6. **FX Gain/Loss**: Stripe USD payout → VND rate ngày nhận (TK 515/635)
          7. **Prepaid Amortization**: Domain/Hosting chia 12 tháng (TK 152 → 642)
          8. **Tax Accrual**: Net Profit x 20% → TK 3331 (TNCN payable)
          9. **P&L Generation**: Revenue - COGS - Opex = Net Profit
          10. **Dashboard Update**: Notion KPIs (MRR, GMV, Take rate, Gross Margin, Net Margin, Runway)

          - **Khấu trừ chi phí**: Chỉ trừ được chi phí **liên quan trực tiếp** tạo ra thu nhập (Platform fee, Marketing, Tool subscription, Internet %, Điện %, Khấu hao laptop). **KHÔNG** trừ: Ăn uống cá nhân, Du lịch, Quà tặng, Phạt.

          - **Stripe/Momo/VNPay payout**: Doanh thu = Gross amount (trước phí). Chi phí = Platform fee.
          - **FX Gain/Loss**: Stripe payout USD → VND tại ngày nhận. Chênh lệch rate ghi nhận TK 515/635.

          ## 8. Unit Economics Dashboard (Monthly)
          | Metric | Formula | Target | Current |
          |--------|---------|--------|---------|
          | **GMV** | Sum(session value) | 100tr/tháng (T3) | |
          | **Take Rate** | Revenue / GMV | 15% | 15% |
          | **Revenue (Net)** | GMV x Take Rate - Platform Fees | 15tr/tháng (T3) | |
          | **Gross Margin** | (Revenue - Platform Fees - Payment Fees) / Revenue | > 85% | |
          | **CAC (Blended)** | Marketing Spend / New Mentee | < 200k | |
          | **LTV (Mentee)** | Avg Sessions x Avg Value x Take Rate x Retention Months | > 2tr | |
          | **Payback Period** | CAC / (Monthly Gross Profit per User) | < 3 tháng | |
          | **Net Margin** | Net Profit / Revenue | > 40% (Solo) | |
          | **Runway** | (Cash + Tax Savings) / Monthly Burn | > 12 tháng | |

          ## 9. When to Incorporate (Chuyển sang Công Ty)

          - [ ] Cần **liability protection** (Contract lớn, Enterprise client)
          - [ ] Cần **raise investment** (Investor chỉ invest vào Company)
          - [ ] **Brand credibility** (Enterprise sales, Government contract)
          - [ ] **Tax optimization** (Company tax 20% vs Personal 20% but more deductibles, Dividend 5% vs Salary progressive)

          1. Hộ gia đình (Simple, tax similar individual) → 2. TNHH 1 TV (Liability protection, separate legal entity) → 3. TNHH 2+ TV / Cổ phần (Investment ready)

          - **Fixed Cost/Tháng**: 5tr VNĐ
          - **Target Margin**: 50% Net Margin
          - **Calculation**: At 50 sessions/tháng (GMV 25tr @ 500k/session) → Variable cost 1.25tr → Total cost 6.25tr → Revenue needed 12.5tr → Take rate = 12.5/25 = **50%**
          - **Verdict**: ❌ **Quá cao** (MentorCruise 20%, ADPList 0%). Chỉ áp dụng khi monopoly.

          ### Model B: Competitor-Based (Market Rate)
          - **Global Benchmark**: MentorCruise 20%, ADPList 0%, MentorMesh 15-20%
          - **VN Benchmark**: Offline coaching 500k-2tr/hr (no platform), Free FB groups
          - **Positioning**: "VN-focused, Vetted, Local payment, Affordable"
          - **Sweet Spot**: **15-20% Take Rate** (Đúng MentorCruise low-end, hơn ADPList value)
          - **Verdict**: ✅ **Viable** - Align với market expectation

          ### Model C: Value-Based (Willingness to Pay)
          - **Mentee Value**: Salary increase 5-10tr/tháng x 12 tháng = 60-120tr/năm. Session cost 500k. **ROI 100-200x**.
          - **WTP (Willingness to Pay)**: Survey beta: "Mức tối đa bạn chi/buổi?" → Median 500k, Max 1tr.
          - **Mentor Value**: Side income 5-10tr/tháng. Time cost 4h/tháng. **Effective rate 1.25-2.5tr/hr**.
          - **Platform Value**: Trust (vetting), Payment guarantee, Scheduling, Video, Review system.
          - **Optimal Take Rate**: **15% Mentee side + 5% Mentor side = 20% Total** (Split fee model)
          - **Verdict**: ✅ **Best long-term** - Capture value created

          ## 3. Unit Economics Model (Month 6 Target: 200 sessions/tháng)

          ### Assumptions
          - Avg Session Value: 500k VNĐ
          - Take Rate: 15% (Blended)
          - Platform Variable Cost: 5% GMV
          - Marketing: 10tr/tháng (Content + Ads + Referral)
          - Fixed Ops: 5tr/tháng

          ### Calculations
          | Metric | Formula | Value |
          |--------|---------|-------|
          | **GMV/Tháng** | 200 sessions x 500k | **100tr VNĐ** |
          | **Revenue/Tháng** | GMV x 15% | **15tr VNĐ** |
          | **Variable Cost** | GMV x 5% | **5tr VNĐ** |
          | **Gross Profit** | Revenue - Variable Cost | **10tr VNĐ** |
          | **Gross Margin** | Gross Profit / Revenue | **66.7%** |
          | **Fixed Cost** | Platform + Marketing + Admin | **15tr VNĐ** |
          | **Net Profit** | Gross Profit - Fixed Cost | **-5tr VNĐ (Loss)** |
          | **Net Margin** | Net Profit / Revenue | **-33%** |

          ### Per Unit (Per Session)
          | Metric | Value |
          |--------|-------|
          | Revenue/Session | 75k VNĐ |
          | Variable Cost/Session | 25k VNĐ |
          | **Contribution Margin/Session** | **50k VNĐ** |
          | Fixed Cost/Session (at 200) | 75k VNĐ |
          | **Break-even Sessions** | Fixed Cost / Contribution Margin = 15tr / 50k = **300 sessions/tháng** |

          ### CAC & LTV
          | Channel | CAC | LTV (12 tháng) | LTV/CAC | Payback |
          |---------|-----|----------------|---------|---------|
          | Organic/SEO | 50k | 1.2tr (24 sessions x 50k) | 24x | 1 session |

          | FB Ads | 300k | 800k | 2.7x | 6 sessions |
          | **Blended** | **120k** | **1.1tr** | **9.2x** | **2.4 sessions** |

          **LTV Calculation**: Avg 2 sessions/tháng x 6 tháng retention x 50k contribution = 600k. + Referral value = ~1.1tr.
